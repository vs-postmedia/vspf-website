Events

The Vancouver Street Photography Festival is a three-day photography festival centered around a curated exhibition of international street photography on display in downtown Vancouver. The schedule will include guest speakers, panel discussions, workshops and photowalks in partnership with local photographers and photo clubs. The exhibition and all events will open to the public at no cost.

Full programme details — speakers, panelists, workshop leaders, venues, programming — will be announced in early 2027.

Produced by the Vancouver Street Photography Festival Society · 2555 Trinity St., Vancouver, B.C., Canada Non-profit incorporation No. S0084372
